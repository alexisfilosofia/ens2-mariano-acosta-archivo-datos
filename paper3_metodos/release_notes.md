# Release notes: v1.0-paper3

## Paper 3 - Reproducible methodological materials v1.0

This release publishes public, aggregate, and reproducible methodological materials for the article project "Del folio a la evidencia: un protocolo reproducible para registros escolares manuscritos".

## Scope

- Period: 1910-1915.
- Corpus: 1.438 registration records.
- Independent validation sample: 180 records.
- Validation object: structured transcription, contextual resolution, normalization, QA, and human validation pipeline.
- Public materials: documentation, aggregate validation tables, sanitized reproducibility notebook, dependency file, privacy audit, and SHA-256 manifest.

## Main aggregate results

- 1.980 conceptual comparisons.
- 1.867 semantic/resolved matches.
- 94,29% semantic/resolved agreement.
- 94,41% agreement weighted by annual corpus composition.
- 95% bootstrap interval: 92,70%-95,98%.
- Sensitivity excluding 295 empty-empty comparisons: 93,29%.
- Double review: 60 records and 1.080 substantive comparisons.
- Exact agreement between independent reviewers: 100% on the double-review aggregate.
- Repetition/IDEM marks evaluated: 398.
- Correctly resolved by the pipeline: 397.

## Privacy and restrictions

Only aggregate results and reproducible documentation are published. Identifiable records, exact addresses, individual coordinates, archival images, full validation spreadsheets, and row-level discrepancies remain restricted.

No DOI is declared in this repository unless and until a real Zenodo DOI is minted.
